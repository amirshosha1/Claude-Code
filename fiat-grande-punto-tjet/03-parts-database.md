## Rules

1. Nothing moves from `ON HOLD` to `NEEDS ORDERING` without a diagnosis note in the relevant job card.
2. Every purchase: record invoice photo reference, supplier, warranty terms, and update [`04-purchase-tracker.md`](04-purchase-tracker.md).
3. Counterfeit alert (Egypt): verify Gates/SKF/NGK/Bosch anti-fake QR codes; buy from authorized distributors where possible.
4. Estirad (imported used) is acceptable ONLY for: body panels, interior trim, alternator/starter cores, EPS column (tested) — **never** rubber, belts, brake friction, bearings, or filters.
