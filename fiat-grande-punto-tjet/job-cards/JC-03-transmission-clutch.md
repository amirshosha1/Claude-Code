## 1. Clutch Kit — 🟢 DONE @ 125k · monitor only

No action this cycle; typical remaining life 60–100k km. Reference below kept for the next replacement.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Symptoms to check | Slip in 5th @2,000 rpm WOT uphill, high bite point, judder, heavy pedal, release-bearing chirp |
| Root cause | Normal friction wear; 155k on original = living on borrowed time, but T-Jet clutches can pass 180k with gentle use |
| Inspection | Road test above. **Do not replace preventively** — it's a gearbox-out job, wait for symptoms, but budget for it |
| Decision | Inspect First → when done, replace **complete kit + release bearing**; check flywheel surface (T-Jet 120 typically solid flywheel — verify; 6-sp/155hp variants may differ) |
| OEM numbers | via ePER by VIN (kit ~55212224 family — confidence Low, must verify) |
| Brands | **Valeo ★★★★★ (usually OE)** · LuK ★★★★★ · Sachs ★★★★★ · Exedy ★★★★☆ · unbranded ☆ never on a turbo |
| Pricing (EGP, LOW) | Kit Valeo/LuK 6,000–10,000 · Labor (box out) 2,500–4,500 · **Total ≈ 9,000–14,500** |
| Risk if ignored | Slipping clutch glazes flywheel (+EGP 2–4k machining/replacement) and can strand you mid-traffic. Worst case: friction material disintegrates, car immobile |

## 3. Gear Linkage & Bushings — 🟡 · **Inspect First**

Sloppy/vague lever = worn linkage bushes (cheap plastic). Pricing (LOW): bush kit 300–800 + labor 300–600.

## 4. Driveshafts & CV Joints — 🟠 · **Inspect First**

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Symptoms | Click on full-lock circles (outer CV), vibration on acceleration 80–110 km/h (inner tripod), torn boots |
| Decision | Torn boot + no noise = re-boot & regrease (cheap). Clicking = replace joint/shaft |
| Brands | GKN/Löbro ★★★★★ (OE) · SKF ★★★★☆ · Metelli ★★★☆☆ · no-name shafts ☆ (vibrate) |
| Pricing (EGP, LOW) | Boot kit 300–600 + labor 500–900 per side · Outer CV 1,500–3,000 · Complete shaft GKN 4,000–7,000 · Estirad shaft 1,200–2,500 (inspect boots + play) |
| Risk if ignored | Dry joint seizes → shaft failure = no drive; can damage gearbox output seal |
