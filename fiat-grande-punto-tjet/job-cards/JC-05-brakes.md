## 2. Rear Discs/Drums + Pads/Shoes — 🟡 · **Inspect First**

Same logic. Rear wears slower; often just needs cleaning/adjustment + handbrake cable lube.
Pricing (LOW): rear discs pair 2,200–4,000 · pads 1,200–2,200 · labor 500–800.

## 3. Brake Fluid — 🔴 · **Must Replace** (unknown age)

DOT 4, full flush ~1 L. Hygroscopic fluid + Egyptian heat = vapor-lock fade risk.
Brands: ATE ★★★★★ · Bosch ★★★★★ · TRW ★★★★★. Pricing: fluid 250–500 + flush labor 300–600. **Every 2 years from now.**

## 4. Flexible Hoses — 🟠 · **Inspect First (age-expired candidates)**

16-year-old rubber: check ballooning/cracks. Replace all 4 if any fails. TRW/ATE ★★★★★. 350–700 each + labor/bleed 600–1,000.
Risk: hose burst = sudden circuit loss 🔴.

## 5. Calipers & Sliders — 🟠 · **Service, not replace**

Full service: strip sliders, new grease, check pistons/boots. EGP 400–800/axle labor + slider kits 200–500.
Only Confirmed seized piston → refurb caliper (TRW/ATE ~2,500–4,500 or local rebuild 800–1,500).

## 6. ABS Sensors & Rings — 🟡 · **Inspect First**

Only on DTC evidence. Bosch/ATE ★★★★★. Sensor 600–1,400 + labor 300–500.

## 7. Handbrake — 🟡

Adjust ≤ 6–8 clicks; cables (pair 800–1,600 + labor 500–900) only if seized/frayed.
