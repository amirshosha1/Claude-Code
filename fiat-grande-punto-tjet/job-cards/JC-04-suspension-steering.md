# JC-04 — Suspension & Steering

**Context:** 155,000 km on Egyptian roads — statistically the most worn system on the car. Front: MacPherson. Rear: twist beam. Steering: **Dualdrive electric (column motor)**.
**Status:** Awaiting shake-down inspection + alignment printout.

---

## 1. Front Lower Control Arms (bushes + ball joints) — 🟠 HIGH · **Likely Failure**

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Symptoms to check | Clunk over bumps, vague steering, inner-edge tire wear, wandering under braking |
| Inspection | Pry-bar on bushes, lever ball joint under load |
| Decision | At this age/roads: replace **complete arms in pairs** (bush + joint integrated) rather than pressing bushes |
| OEM numbers | via ePER by VIN (LH/RH arm ~ 50703069/50703070 family — confidence Low, verify) |
| Brands | **Lemförder ★★★★★** · TRW ★★★★★ · Febi ★★★★☆ · Metelli/Frap ★★★☆☆ · Egyptian no-name ☆ (fail in months) |
| Pricing (EGP, LOW) | Arm 2,000–3,800 each (TRW/Lemförder) · Labor pair 800–1,500 · + alignment 400–700 · **Total pair ≈ 5,500–9,500** |
| Risk if ignored | Ball joint separation at speed = loss of control 🔴. Worn bushes eat tires (EGP 4–8k/pair) |

## 2. Anti-roll Bar Links + Bushes — 🟠 · **Likely Failure** (cheap)

Knock/rattle over small bumps. Links: TRW/Lemförder/Febi ★★★★★~☆. Pricing: link 400–900 each, D-bush 150–400 each, labor 300–600.

## 3. Front Struts + Top Mounts + Bearings — 🟠 · **Inspect First (Likely)**

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Symptoms | Leaking oil on strut body = Confirmed. Clunk turning at standstill = top mount bearing. Floaty ride, long rebound |
| Decision | Replace in **pairs**, always with new top mounts + bearings + bump stops/boots |
| Brands | Shocks: **Sachs ★★★★★** · KYB Excel-G ★★★★★ · Monroe ★★★★☆ · Magneti Marelli ★★★☆☆. Mounts/bearings: SNR ★★★★★ · SKF ★★★★★ · Febi ★★★★☆ |
| Pricing (EGP, LOW) | Strut 2,500–4,000 each (KYB/Sachs) · Mount+bearing kit 700–1,400/side · Labor pair 1,000–1,800 · + alignment · **Front axle total ≈ 8,500–13,500** |
| Risk if ignored | Braking distance grows sharply (shocks control tire contact); cups tires; stresses new arms |

## 4. Rear Shocks + Spring Seats — 🟡 · **Inspect First**

Same brand logic. Pricing: shock 1,800–3,000 each · labor pair 500–900. Twist-beam pivot bushes: inspect (clunk from rear over bumps) — pressing labor 800–1,500 + bushes 600–1,200 (Lemförder/Febi).

## 5. Wheel Bearings — 🟡 · **Inspect First**

Hum changing with lane-weave load = Confirmed side. Front hub-bearing unit w/ ABS ring: **SKF ★★★★★** · SNR ★★★★★ · FAG ★★★★★.
Pricing: bearing 1,500–2,800 + labor 600–1,200 per side. Risk: seizure/wheel wobble, ABS faults.

## 6. Track Rod Ends + Inner Tie Rods — 🟠 · **Inspect First (Likely)**

Play at 3–9 o'clock wheel rock. TRW/Lemförder ★★★★★. Ends 500–1,000 each, inners 700–1,200 each, labor 400–800 + alignment.
Risk: alignment never holds, tire wear, vague straight-line.

## 7. Dualdrive Electric Power Steering — 🟡 · **Inspect First — expensive, diagnose carefully**

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Known faults | Intermittent assist loss, "steering" warning, torque-sensor faults in column unit |
| Inspection | MultiECUScan EPS fault read FIRST. Check battery/alternator voltage (low voltage mimics EPS failure!). Check column connector corrosion |
| Decision | Only after Confirmed diagnosis: repaired/exchange column (specialist rebuild ≈ 4,000–8,000) vs Estirad used column (2,500–6,000, gamble) vs new (very expensive, rarely justified) |
| Risk if ignored | Sudden assist loss is manageable but dangerous in traffic; torque sensor faults can cause uneven assist |

## 8. Alignment — 🟠 · **Must Do** after any of the above

Full 4-wheel alignment with printout. EGP 400–800. Non-negotiable finisher for this job card.
